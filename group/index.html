SPERC and Carbon Capture & Biomass Utilisation (CCBU)

Wan Azlina leads the Sustainable Process Engineering Research Centre (SPERC). Dr. Mohamad Rezi Abdul Hamid leads CCBU, one of its research groups.

CCBU studies sustainable biomass valorisation through four integrated areas: pretreatment, conversion, product recovery and assessment. Environmentally responsible pretreatment improves biomass reactivity, followed by optimised thermochemical and biochemical conversion. Separation and upgrading recover biofuels, chemicals and materials for industrial use. Techno-economic and environmental assessments, including life-cycle analysis and cost modelling, evaluate process feasibility and sustainability. These areas support scalable, circular technologies that turn biomass waste into useful products.
