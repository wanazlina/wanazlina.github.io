About

Prof. Ir. Dr. Wan Azlina Wan Ab Karim Ghani is a chemical engineer at Universiti Putra Malaysia who turns waste and biomass into energy, carbon materials and practical environmental solutions.

She joined UPM as a tutor in 2000, completed a PhD in combustion engineering at the University of Sheffield in 2005, and spent 2008 to 2012 as a postdoctoral and visiting scholar at the University of Melbourne. She became Professor in 2020 and heads the Sustainable Process Engineering Research Centre (SPERC). Her work covers thermochemical conversion (gasification, pyrolysis, combustion), biochar and carbon materials, carbon capture, utilisation and storage, and the modelling of biomass value chains.

Her group built a 10 kW integrated mobile gasifier and the Biochar Microwave Carbonizer, and modelled Malaysia's oil-palm value chains in the Newton–RCUK BEFEW nexus project with UKM, UTP, UNMC and the University of Bath. She serves on the Department of Environment's EIA review panel and Standards Malaysia's TC 238 Solid Biofuels committee, and brings circular-economy thinking to schools and communities through STEM and SULAM service-learning.
