Research

Her work follows waste from where it arises to what it can become: mapping biomass and waste resources, converting them by heat, turning the products into fuels and materials, and using those materials to clean water, air and flue gas.

Research themes as a process flow

  1. 1. Feedstock Biomass resources and waste systems Where the residue is, and how to collect and manage it
  2. 2. Conversion Gasification, pyrolysis and combustion Turning biomass and waste into gas, oil and solid fuel
  3. 3. Product Fuels, chemicals and materials from biomass Biochar, catalysts, biofuels and electrode materials
  4. 4. Impact Cleaner water, air and climate Adsorbents and capture for dyes, metals, acid gases and CO₂

1. Feedstock

Biomass resources and waste systems

The problem

Malaysia generates large volumes of oil-palm residue, food waste and municipal solid waste. Much of it is burned, dumped or landfilled, and the energy and materials it holds are lost.

How she works on it

Satellite and machine-learning mapping of oil-palm land; value-chain and food–energy–water nexus optimisation; anaerobic digestion and biogas trials on food waste, manure and oily sludge; decision tools such as P-graph and the DeMI interface for municipal solid waste; behavioural studies of household food-waste separation.

2. Conversion

Gasification, pyrolysis and combustion

The problem

Biomass and waste hold useful energy, but burning them as they are is inefficient and polluting. Engineered thermal conversion recovers that energy as clean gas, oil or upgraded solid fuel.

How she works on it

Fluidised-bed air and steam gasification for hydrogen-rich gas from empty fruit bunches, palm kernel shell and Napier grass; pyrolysis and thermogravimetric kinetics; co-combustion with coal; microwave and hydrothermal carbonisation to biochar and biocoal; process modelling in Aspen Plus and computational fluid dynamics.

In the record

53 Scopus papers in this theme (2009–2026), cited 2,114 times; 24 of them in Q1 journals.

3. Product

Fuels, chemicals and materials from biomass

The problem

Fuels, chemicals and advanced materials still depend mostly on fossil sources. Residues from farms and food processing can replace some of them.

How she works on it

Synthesis of biochar, hydrochar and activated carbon; heterogeneous catalysts for biodiesel and glycerol conversion; microalgae and lignocellulose biorefining; carbon foams, electrodes and cellulose hydrogels for supercapacitors and flow batteries.

In the record

4. Impact

Cleaner water, air and climate

The problem

Dyes, heavy metals, acid gases and carbon dioxide from industry damage water, air and the climate. Treatment often relies on costly, imported materials.

How she works on it

Low-cost adsorbents from agricultural waste for dyes and metals; activated-carbon monoliths for simultaneous SO₂ and NOx removal from flue gas; H₂S and CO₂ adsorption; biochar for carbon sequestration; mineral carbonation of mining waste and steel slag for carbon storage; microalgae bioremediation of aquaculture wastewater.

In the record

43 Scopus papers in this theme (2011–2026), cited 3,396 times; 14 of them in Q1 journals.
