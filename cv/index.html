Summary

Prof. Ir. Dr. Wan Azlina Wan Ab Karim Ghani is a chemical engineer at Universiti Putra Malaysia who turns waste and biomass into energy, carbon materials and practical environmental solutions. Her group built a 10 kW integrated mobile gasifier and the Biochar Microwave Carbonizer, and modelled Malaysia's oil-palm value chains in the Newton–RCUK BEFEW nexus project with UKM, UTP, UNMC and the University of Bath. She serves on the Department of Environment's EIA review panel and Standards Malaysia's TC 238 Solid Biofuels committee, and brings circular-economy thinking to schools and communities through STEM and SULAM service-learning.
